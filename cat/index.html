CAT is a computer-based test held once a year, in late November, with three sections — Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC), Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR), and Quantitative Ability (QA) — each with its own sectional time limit within the overall 2-hour test. Every part of your preparation here is built around this exact structure.
