IITs are Institutes of National Importance, funded entirely by the Central Government and created as India's answer to MIT and Caltech. What sets them apart isn't just teaching quality — it's the calibre of the peer group, the research culture, and a global alumni network that has founded over 2,000 companies, including Flipkart and Sun Pharma.

Tamil Nadu tip

IIT admissions — quick answers

Are the newer IITs (2008 onwards) worth attending? +

Yes, with a caveat — placements and infrastructure are still maturing at the 16 IITs founded since 2008 compared to the original seven. But the rule of thumb holds: a new IIT's CSE or EE branch is still a stronger practical choice than a non-CS branch at an old IIT. A JEE Advanced rank of 8,000–15,000 typically falls in the new-IIT zone for non-CS branches.

Why is IIT Madras the top choice for Tamil Nadu students specifically? +

Beyond being India's #1 ranked institution (NIRF 2024), IIT Madras is physically in Chennai — meaning TN students can visit family easily, tap into the strongest regional alumni network of any IIT, and sit next to India's largest university tech park (IITM Research Park). It also has the largest number of TN students among all IITs.

Do SC/ST students really pay zero IIT tuition? +

Yes — 100% tuition fee waiver at every IIT for SC/ST students, who pay only mess and hostel charges (roughly ₹1.2–1.5 lakh/year). Combined with the central Post-Matric Scholarship for maintenance, this makes an IIT education close to free for many families. See our Scholarships hub for the full breakdown.
